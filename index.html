About Samda Hospital

Advance Healthcare in Rajnandgaon

Established in 2021, Samda Superspeciality Hospital was founded with a singular mission: to eliminate the need for patients in Rajnandgaon and surrounding districts to travel to Raipur or Nagpur for critical surgeries.

Under the guidance of senior surgeon Dr. B.C. Jain and superspecialists Dr. Romil Jain (Surgical Gastroenterology) and Dr. Nikhar Jain (Urology & Andrology), our 45-bed facility combines compassionate nursing care with cutting-edge surgical infrastructure.

Zero Compromise on Patient Safety

Strict infection control protocols, modular OTs, and dedicated post-operative recovery units.

Transparent Pricing & Cashless Schemes

No hidden charges, clear surgical package pricing, and dedicated insurance helpdesk.

Serving Whole Western Chhattisgarh

Conveniently located for patients across Rajnandgaon, Durg, Dongargarh, Balod, and Khairagarh.

PM-JAY & CASHLESS HELP DESK

Free & Cashless Treatment Under Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

Samda Superspeciality Hospital is an officially empanelled healthcare provider under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (Dr. Khubchand Baghel Swasthya Sahayata Yojana). Eligible patients can avail free surgical treatments up to ₹5,00,000.

4 Simple Steps to Avail Free Treatment

1

Bring Your Documents

Ayushman Card / Ration Card (BPL/APL) + Patient's Aadhaar Card.

2

Ayushman Mitra Verification

Our in-house Ayushman Helpdesk verifies eligibility on the PM-JAY portal within 10 minutes.

3

Doctor Consultation & Pre-Auth

Specialist diagnosis, treatment package booking, and instant digital approval.

4

Cashless treatment confirmation

Coverage, approval, limits, and eligible treatments vary. Confirm all benefits with the hospital helpdesk before admission.
