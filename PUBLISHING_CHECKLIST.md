# Samda Hospital pre-publication checklist

This checklist must be completed by an authorised hospital representative before the final domain launch.

## Clinical and operational sign-off

- [ ] Every doctor name, photo, designation, qualification, specialty, department, language and OPD timing is current and approved in writing.
- [ ] All procedures, facility descriptions, ICU/OT details, ambulance statements and service-hours statements are factually verified.
- [ ] Ayushman PM-JAY, insurer and TPA information is confirmed with the relevant hospital desk. Do not imply guaranteed coverage.
- [ ] Emergency number, reception number, email, physical address, maps link and WhatsApp number have been tested on a mobile phone.
- [ ] All reviews, patient stories, photos and videos have written hospital approval and any required patient consent.

## Patient experience and privacy

- [ ] Hindi translations for Emergency, OPD, Ayushman, departments and contact options are approved by the hospital.
- [ ] Booking Mitra is reviewed as an availability-and-callback request only; it must not be described as online appointment booking.
- [ ] A staff member is assigned to respond to WhatsApp availability and callback requests.
- [ ] The privacy policy, consent wording and emergency warning are approved by hospital management.

## Search and measurement

- [ ] Set the final domain in canonical URLs, structured data, `robots.txt`, sitemap and Sanity CORS settings.
- [ ] Create Google Search Console and submit the final sitemap.
- [ ] Claim or create the hospital's Google Business Profile and ensure its name, address, phone and hours match the website.
- [ ] Create an approved analytics property and provide its measurement ID before adding tracking. Track only consented, aggregate events such as call-button and WhatsApp-button clicks; do not send health details or form text to analytics.
- [ ] Create dedicated doctor and department SEO pages only after the hospital has approved the source data and URLs.

## Performance

- [ ] Review every uploaded gallery and doctor image for consent, accuracy and compression before publishing.
- [ ] Keep the hero image and each gallery image appropriately sized for mobile use; test the public site on a slower mobile connection.
