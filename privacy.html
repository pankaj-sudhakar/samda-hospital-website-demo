Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This website is an information and appointment-request service for Samda Superspeciality Hospital. It does not store appointment form entries on this website.

Information you choose to share

When you select “Continue to WhatsApp”, the contact and appointment details you entered are placed into a WhatsApp message addressed to Samda Hospital. This information may include your name, phone number, requested department, appointment preference, and any information you choose to add.

How it is used

Hospital staff may use this information only to respond to your enquiry, coordinate an appointment, or provide related patient support. Do not include payment information, identity numbers, or more medical detail than is needed for your request.

Third parties

WhatsApp is operated by Meta and has its own privacy practices. By continuing to WhatsApp, you agree to WhatsApp processing the message under its terms. This site also uses Google Fonts and Font Awesome to display its design.

Your choices

You can contact the hospital by phone instead of using a form. For a privacy question, correction, or deletion request, contact samdahospital@gmail.com.

Important

This form is not for emergencies. For urgent care, call the hospital emergency line or local emergency services immediately.
